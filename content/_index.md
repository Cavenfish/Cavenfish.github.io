# About Me

I'm a Brazilian-American who was born in Orlando, FL, USA, but lived for part of my childhood in São Paulo, SP, BR. I got my BS & MS in physics at the University of Central Florida, USA. After that I went to the Netherlands where I completed my PhD in theoretical chemistry, doing research on atomistic simulations of vibrational energy dissipation.

During my PhD, I wrote an atomic simulation suite in Julia, which helped me realize my passion for software engineering. Now I'm a software engineer at Impulse Space in Los Angeles, CA, USA, working on code that automates the physical testing of our spacecraft.

My transition from theoretical chemistry to software engineering might seem strange, but there are actually a lot of talented software engineers in science. Unfortunately, they mostly use Fortran 77, but they are still talented nonetheless lol.
