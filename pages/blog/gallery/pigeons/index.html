    Pigeons are intelligent and loving birds that have been domesticated by humans. They have served as messengers, spies, breast cancer detectors, shipwreck spotters, and sadly as the guidance system for missiles. Despite how gentle they are and all the value they have brought humanity, most people nowadays dislike and mistreat them.
