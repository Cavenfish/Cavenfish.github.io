# Pigeon Pictures

Pigeons are intelligent and loving birds that have been domesticated by humans.
They have served as [messengers](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Homing_pigeon),
[spies](https://www.cia.gov/stories/story/natural-spies-animals-in-espionage/),
[breast cancer detetors](https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC4651348/),
[ship wreck spotters](https://www.washingtonpost.com/archive/politics/1979/07/31/in-sea-rescues-the-eagle-eyes-are-pigeons/3d5f4488-1252-4858-86ec-f05204a7162e/),
and sadly as the [guidance system for missles](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Project_Pigeon).
Despite how gentle they are and all the value they have brought humanity, most
people nowadays dislike and mistreat them.

I find them beautiful, and enjoy wathcing them and taking pictures of them. Below
are some of the pictures I've taken over the years of some beautiful pigeons.
